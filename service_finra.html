What is FINRA Fingerprinting?

FINRA (Financial Industry Regulatory Authority) fingerprinting is a mandatory statutory background verification process for individuals registering with FINRA member firms in the United States.

Under SEC Rule 17f-2, candidate fingerprints are submitted to conduct an FBI criminal background check as part of federal regulatory requirements. Candidates must have their friction ridges rolled on authentic FINRA-approved cards with authentic employer codes and CRD numbers.
